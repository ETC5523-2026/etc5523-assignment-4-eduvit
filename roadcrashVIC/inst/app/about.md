#### How to use this app

**Victoria:** crashes across the state for the selected years, metric and scale; all charts and the map follow these selections.

**My area:** crashes in one or more local government areas (LGAs) for the selected months, including the location of each crash on the map.

Maps can be zoomed and moved; hovering over an area or a crash shows its details.

#### Definitions

**Crash:** a road crash in which at least one person was injured or killed, as recorded by Victoria Police.

**KSI:** people killed or seriously injured.

**Per 1,000 crashes:** people killed or injured for every 1,000 crashes; a measure of how severe crashes were.

**Per 100,000 residents:** yearly number of crashes, or of people killed or injured, for every 100,000 people living in the area.

**Index (first year = 100):** each year's value relative to the first selected year, set to 100. It is included to compare crash trends with Victoria's strong population growth, and is not a standard road-safety measure.

#### Things to keep in mind

- Rates per resident include every crash within an area, including those involving visitors and through traffic.
- Small areas and short periods have few crashes, so their figures can vary considerably by chance.
- Crash numbers in 2020 and 2021 were affected by COVID-19 restrictions.
- The location of part of the crashes from late 2025 has not been recorded yet. These crashes are included in Victorian totals, but not in the maps or in LGA figures.
- Alpine resorts and islands not administered by a council are grouped as `Unincorporated Vic`. Their resident population is small compared with the number of visitors, so no rate is shown.
